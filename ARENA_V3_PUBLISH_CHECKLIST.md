# Arena v3 site: publish checklist

Branch `arena-v3-site`. It changes the Game Arena pages of the GitHub Pages site (`site/`). Nothing on this branch has
been pushed or published. Work through every box before merging to `main`, which is what publishes the site.

## What changed
- `site/arena/` is now **Game Arena v3**, the current benchmark. It holds the leaderboard, how to read it, survival
  curves, the three games with clips, latency, rules, caveats, the audit trail and an archive of the legacy arenas.
  - Page: `site/arena/index.html`, `v3.css`, `v3.js`.
  - Data: `site/arena/v3.json`, generated by `site/arena/build/make_v3.py` from the Board results.json.
  - The old hub list moved into the page's Archive section. `site/arena/app.js` and `site/arena/data.json` are old
    leftovers and are kept untouched.
- `site/arena-live/` is kept unchanged as **Arena v2 (legacy)**. It has a legacy banner and a new title, and its data
  and media are untouched.
- `site/build_chrome.py`:
  - nav is now Benchmark · Game Arena (the Arena Live and Replays tabs are gone);
  - the footer has "Game Arena v3" and an "Archive (legacy)" column;
  - every page was re-stamped.
- `site/index.html`: the home card now points to Arena v3, and "first in Arena Live" became "first in Arena v2
  (legacy)".
- Public text says **Burst**, never Chord ("T-dual Burst"). The internal id `t_dual/chord` stays in `v3.json`.
  `make_v3.py` fails if the word "Chord" would appear in any label.

## Known limits (documented on the page, not bugs)
- The site is dark only by brand (`color-scheme: dark` in `assets/nagi.css`). There is no light theme to match.
- The survival charts show two chosen players in colour and the rest in grey. Six coloured series cannot be told
  apart all at once, including under colour-vision deficiency (checked with the dataviz palette validator), so a
  comparison view is used instead.
- Booster Gauntlet: 25 of 60 rounds, including all 10 of T-dual Burst's, ended at exactly 22.0 s, the first hop's
  landing deadline. The page says this, and ties are scored ½. The per-round cause (hop timeout or another failure) is
  in the private records. **Confirm it from the records before publishing**, and if every one is a hop timeout, say
  so plainly. (Confirmed 2026-09-27 from `score.cause`: all 25 were hop timeouts, and none was a crash; the page now says
  so. In the Burst P2 run all 24 of Burst's Booster rounds were hop timeouts too.)
