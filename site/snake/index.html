What the models actually see

Identical explicit sensors for each direction: collision, reachable space (flood fill), distance to food and next-ring safety. Models select UP / RIGHT / DOWN / LEFT. They do not see pixels. Runs are independent trajectories, not simultaneous head-to-head combat.

What latency means

Local models ran serially on one H100; Jev ran through its remote API. Latencies are measured, but the paths and hardware are not matched. Simulation steps wait for decisions: this pilot measures survival quality, not a real-time timeout contest. Replay speed is compressed.
